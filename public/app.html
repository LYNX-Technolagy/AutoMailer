Connect Gmail

AutoMailer sends email through your own Gmail account. Your App Password is stored in server memory for this session only, and is cleared after 1 hour of inactivity.

How do I get an App Password?
  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/security and make sure 2-Step Verification is on. App Passwords only exist if 2FA is enabled.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Type a name — anything, like AutoMailer — and click Create.
  4. Google shows a 16-character password. Copy it. You won't see it again.
  5. Paste it below (spaces are fine — they get stripped).

Not your normal Gmail password. If you see "The setting you are looking for is not available," 2-Step Verification isn't enabled yet.
